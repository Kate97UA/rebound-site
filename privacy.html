Rebound

Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 October 2026

Rebound is a habit tracker with no account, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Your habits stay on your iPhone and in your own iCloud. The only time anything leaves your devices is when you use the optional AI coach, and the app asks you first.

1. What stays on your device and in your iCloud

Your habits, their versions and schedules, the days you log, pauses, check-ins (including any words you type) and settings are stored on your iPhone. If you are signed in to iCloud, they are also kept in your private iCloud database through Apple’s CloudKit, so they sync between your devices. That database belongs to your Apple Account; the developer of Rebound cannot see it. Widgets and the Shortcuts action read and write the same data on your device.

Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server for them.

2. The AI coach (Rebound Pro, optional)

After a missed day, Rebound can ask what got in the way and suggest one small change. With Rebound Pro, that suggestion can be written by an AI. Before the first time, the app asks for your permission, and you can turn it off at any time in Settings → AI coach.

When the AI coach is on and you finish a check-in, the app sends:

  • the habit’s name, its full and tiny versions, schedule, routine anchor and reminder times;
  • a 14-character summary of your last 14 days (for example done, tiny, missed, paused) and the momentum score;
  • the reason you chose and any words you typed (up to 300 characters);
  • the app language, a proof of your Pro purchase (see section 4) and a random app-generated identifier (see section 3).

This goes over an encrypted connection to Rebound’s server, a function hosted by Supabase. The server checks that you have Pro, passes the habit details, reason and words to Anthropic’s Claude model to write one suggestion, checks the answer and sends it back to your iPhone. The server does not store the request or the answer; its logs contain only a status code and how long the request took.

Anthropic processes this data to provide the answer, does not use it to train its models, and keeps it only for a limited time under its commercial terms. Nothing sent is linked to your name, email or Apple Account.

If the AI coach is off, or you don’t have Pro, nothing is sent: the check-in shows a short note written into the app instead.

3. Daily limit

To keep the AI coach fair, each device can ask for a limited number of suggestions per day. The app creates a random identifier when first used; the server stores only a salted hash of it and a count for the current day. It cannot be used to identify you or your device.

4. Purchases

Rebound Pro is sold through the App Store. Apple handles payment; the developer never sees your payment details or Apple Account. To check Pro for the AI coach, the app sends Apple’s signed proof of your purchase (product, dates, transaction number). The server verifies Apple’s signature and does not store it.

5. What Rebound does not do

  • No account, sign-in, name or email.
  • No advertising, no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs, no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No selling or sharing of data, except the AI coach request described above.
  • No access to Apple Health, contacts, photos, location or microphone.

6. Your choices

  • Export: Settings → Your data creates a spreadsheet (CSV) or a full JSON file of everything.
  • AI coach: turn it on or off in Settings.
  • Delete: delete habits in the app. Deleting the app removes the data on that device; to remove the iCloud copy, open the Settings app → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Rebound → Delete Data.

7. Children

Rebound is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date. Changes that send more data anywhere will be asked for in the app first.

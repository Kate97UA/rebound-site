Common questions

What are the full and tiny versions?

The full version is what you aim for on a normal day. The tiny version is the smallest step that still counts on a hard day. Both keep your momentum going.

How does momentum work?

Each habit has a score from 0 to 100. It rises when you show up and falls gently when you miss, so one bad day never wipes out your progress. Tap the score on Today for details.

Can I log a day I forgot?

Yes. On Today, use ‹ to go back up to 6 days and log or correct them.

How do I change the order of my habits?

Tap ⋯ → Reorder habits and drag them. In Settings you can also choose whether done habits move down.

My habits don’t appear on my other device.

Both devices need to be signed in to the same Apple Account with iCloud turned on. Syncing can take a few minutes. Settings in Rebound shows whether iCloud is signed in.

How do I restore my purchase?

Open ⋯ → Rebound Pro and tap Restore Purchases. Subscriptions are managed in the Settings app → your name → Subscriptions.

What does the AI coach send, and can I turn it off?

See the privacy policy. You can turn it off in Settings → AI coach.

How do I get my data out?

Settings → Your data → Export as spreadsheet (CSV) or Export everything (JSON).
